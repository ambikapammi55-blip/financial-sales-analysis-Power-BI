# Financial Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

A multi-page Power BI report that analyzes sales, profit, discounts, and product performance across countries and customer segments.

## Report Pages
- **Sales Overview** – Gross sales, discounts, profit, units sold, sales by country and by month
- **Profit Analysis** – Profit by product, segment, country and discount band
- **Product Performance** – Sales, profit and units sold by product, with a detailed table
- **Sales by Product** – Top 5 and bottom 5 products, sales by country and segment
- **Time Intelligence** – Monthly and yearly sales and profit trends
- **Discount Analysis** – Impact of discounts on sales and profit, discount bands, map view
- **Measures** – KPI cards for all key DAX measures

## Key Measures (DAX)
Total Sales, Total Gross Sales, Total Profit, Total Discounts, Total Units Sold, Total Products, Profit Margin %, Previous Year Sales, YoY Growth %

## Filters (Slicers)
Date, Country, Segment, Product, Discount Band, Month

## Data
Financial sales dataset (`financials` table) with sales, COGS, discounts, profit, segment, country and product details.

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX
- Data modelling and time intelligence

## How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop
3. Use the slicers to explore the data
